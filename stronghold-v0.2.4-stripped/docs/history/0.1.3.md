### 23.1 联防 carries the HP ratio and the SP only — no running skill (community report #34, GitHub #82) — `match/unite.js uniteBattleOpts`, `sim/skills.js` (`reset`, `spTotal`, `setSpTotal`), `sim/Battle.js` (`_buildResult` unitsEnd, `_deploy`, `_startDeploying`), `match/fields.js` (client-reported unitsEnd), `match/PlayerState.js battleInput`

- **Seen** (community report #34 「圣聆初雪2技能联防被吞技能」; GitHub #82): every skill that was running when a helper's own combat ended was already on at the 联防 start, with an empty bar — 圣聆初雪's S2 (a toggle: SP 0 once cast) looked "eaten" although it ran.
- **Official**: PRTS 卫戍协议/帮助 §联防阶段 "部署完成后，将对应单位的生命比例、技力修改至与上一阶段结束时相同（召唤物仅修改技力，上一阶段为退场状态的干员强制退场）"; the official setup message carries per unit only `charBattleStatusList: [{ instId, hp, tech }]` (research 09 §3 `HelpBattleInfo`). The 技力 of a skill that is running: PRTS 技能 "触发技能后，触发技能的单位将消耗相应的技力，随后在技能持续期间进入阻回" — spent at the activation, nothing recovered while it runs. PRTS 技能 "可充能X次…当前技力上限等于该技能技力需求的X倍": a charge skill's 技力 includes its stored charges.
- **Cause**: `unite.js` copied `unitsEnd.skillActive` into `carryState`, and `Skill.reset` re-activated a carried timed skill for free (a fresh duration / ammo, no charge spent). Two more gaps in the carried SP: `unitsEnd.sp` was the SP towards the next charge only, so stored charges were lost (a full 2-charge skill came back with one); summons carried nothing (DESIGN §19.3 had left 召唤物仅修改技力 out).
- **Now**: `carryState` is `{ hpPct, sp }` for an operator, `{ sp }` for a board summon piece, `{ down: true }` as before. `unitsEnd.sp` is `skill.spTotal` (stored charges × cost + the SP towards the next charge); `reset` rebuilds the charges from it. A skill running at the end enters 联防 switched off with the SP it had left — 0 for a one-charge skill — and recharges from there (圣聆初雪 S2: off, SP 0, 60 s to recast — 52 elite; S3 likewise; her S1 with one charge and 5 SP stored comes back with exactly that). `unitsEnd` now also lists the board's summon pieces (a board uid); the server accepts them from a client-reported result (`fields.js`: `own.all`), `unite.js` gives a summon on the field at the end its SP, `battleInput` hands it over, and `_deploy` applies a carried HP ratio to operators only. A board piece that content deploys during the start deployment (a tactician's 流形 / 狼群 comes with its owner's deploy, `initial: false`) takes its carry on that first deployment (`_startDeploying`). The carried SP is set "部署完成后", as PRTS says: `_deploy` applies it in `reset` and sets it again after the `deploy` hook (`skill.setSpTotal`: SP and charges rebuilt, nothing fired, a passive or an already running timed skill left alone), so a deploy-time SP gift does not come on top — a bar that ended at 30 / 40 used to come back ready with 独行 ("初始技力+15") or 黄沙罗盘 ("初始技力+30"); later redeploys in the same battle keep those gifts (initial SP 10 + 15 / + 30). In practice only 缪尔赛思's 流形 (cost 100, initial SP 95) has an SP skill among the placeable summons: it now enters with its end SP. `unitsEnd.skillActive` is still reported, never carried.
- [ASSUMED]: a summon off the field at the end of the own combat enters fresh (its 技力 at the end does not exist). Not changed: the HP ratio is still applied at the deployment (PRTS sets it "部署完成后" too; no deploy-time effect is known to change it).
- **§22.4 needs a word changed**: its "联防 carries no effects (HP, SP and a running skill only)" now reads "(the HP ratio and the SP only)".
- Docs: DESIGN §5.1 `carryState` / unitsEnd comments (pinned in test/docs-consistency.test.js), §19.3's summon sentence, docs/SIM.md §1.1 and the result shape, docs/META.md (联防), docs/PLAYING.md (联防).
- Tests: `test/match/feedback3-unitecarry.test.js` (unite.js carry: HP + SP for an operator, never `skillActive`, `{ sp }` for a summon on the field, nothing for one off it, `down` as before; `battleInput` hands a summon its carry; a real own combat whose 30 s skill still runs at the end → unitsEnd sp 0, skillActive true → the 联防 battle: skill off, no activation, SP from 0, no skill ATK, a 2-charge skill's stored charges back; 缪尔赛思's 流形 with `{ sp: 40, hpPct: 0.3 }`: SP 40 instead of 95, HP full; 独行 and 黄沙罗盘 with a carried 0 / 30 of 40: the bar comes back as it ended, not ready — 15 / 30 more and ready before the fix —, a later redeploy 25 / 40), `test/sim/fidelity.test.js` and `test/sim/skills.test.js` (rewritten: a carried `skillActive` starts nothing), `test/match/{combat,clientCombat,unitedown}.test.js` (the carry shape).

### 23.2 联防: 阿戈尔's devour counts the operators down since their own combat (community report #3, GitHub #33 item 3) — `content/bonds/core.js devour`

- **Seen** (community report #3): "阿戈尔在进协防的时候，即使已经处于撤退状态的干员也应该会在协防阶段时重新参与进阿戈尔的吞噬攻击力计算，不是像现在这样数值被重置" — a member's devour gains vanished in 联防 when an operator of its chain had been knocked out at the end of the own combat.
- **Official / decision**: no text gives the order of the 联防 forced exit and the battle-start devour (§19.3, §22.3: [ASSUMED] forced exit first). **Per players' reports (two players), owner's decision 2026-10-04**: in 联防 the operators that were down at the end of their own phase still take part in the battle-start devour — marking order, the unit in front, base ATK / block gains — and are then forced out.
- **Cause**: `Battle.start` forces those operators out right before `battleStart`; `devour()` took only units on the field, so the chain broke at the down operator's tile: 乌尔比安 → 幽灵鲨 (down) → 歌蕾蒂娅 → 隐现 gave 乌尔比安 nothing (an empty tile in front of him).
- **Now** (the smallest change; the forced exit keeps its place, so no other `battleStart` handler — devices, 征召's row check, 炎, the add-on refresh, kits, garrisons, bands — sees anything new): `devour()` treats an operator forced out at this start (`carryState.down`, removal reason `FORCED_EXIT`, lying on its tile) as standing for the whole devour but what would land on it. It marks in its turn (left first, then top), it is found as "the unit in front" (`battle.downOn`; the chain goes on through it when it is a member), its base ATK / block count for its marker, and its own marks resolve: their 物理流失 lands on its targets, a kill is credited to it as usual, a member it knocks out spends a 5-tier revive like any first knock-out (whose pending marks are then cancelled). Only the marks on it resolve nothing (`knocked`: it is off the field) — it is never knocked out again, never revived, never devoured (no layers, no `deaths`). Its own marking gains are a persistent buff, as for every marker since 0.1.0: it has them when it redeploys. Measured (real data): the chain above (3 members) gives 乌尔比安 +1569 ATK (幽灵鲨 530 + 歌蕾蒂娅 640 + 隐现 399) and +5 block — exactly the gains with 幽灵鲨 standing; 幽灵鲨 lies forced out with no knock-out, no revive, no 物理流失 on her; 歌蕾蒂娅 and 隐现 fall to 乌尔比安's marks as with her standing (her own marks find them knocked out already). With 幽灵鲨 (down) at the head of 幽灵鲨 → 歌蕾蒂娅 → 隐现, her two marks knock both out, exactly as standing (3 members: same knock-outs; 5 members: 歌蕾蒂娅's revive is spent and she stands again at full HP).
- [ASSUMED]: nothing lands on a down operator (it is forced out right after; a 物理流失 on it could only knock it out a second time or spend a revive), so — unlike a standing one that an earlier mark knocks out — a down marker always resolves its own marks. **Review fix (Grok, round 4)**: the first version skipped the down marker's own marks too, so the unit in front of it took no 物理流失 and a 5-tier revive stayed unspent while it still passed its base ATK / block on.
- **§22.3 needs a word changed**: its "The order of the 联防 forced exit and the battle-start devour stays [ASSUMED] (§19.3: forced exit first); no source gives it" now points here. §19.3's [ASSUMED] sentence names the exception (edited in place).
- Tests: `test/content/feedback3-egir-down.test.js` (the chain 乌尔比安 → 幽灵鲨 (down) → 歌蕾蒂娅 → 隐现 in a 联防 battle: 乌尔比安's devour buff equals the chain with 幽灵鲨 standing; she is forced out — no `'killed'` death, no redeploy, no 物理流失 on her, one knock-out fewer in `deaths`; her marking gains kept; 幽灵鲨 (down) at the head of the chain with 3 and with 5 members: the resolved marks, kills, knock-outs and — at 5 — 歌蕾蒂娅's spent revive equal the chain with her standing, and she stays forced out); `test/content/bonds_core.test.js` unchanged and passing.

### 23.4 A stunned operator keeps its natural SP recovery (community report #18, 洛洛 S2) — `sim/skills.js tick`

- **Seen** (community report #18): "洛洛二技能结束眩晕好像不会自然恢复技力，而且好像持续时间也不太对，洛洛二技能是有过载的".
- **Official**: PRTS 技能 — natural recovery is a 1 s SP cooldown slot, and "在阻回状态或技力条已满时，保留剩余冷却时间，计时暂停": only 阻回 (SP_RECOVER_STOPPED, "停止技力自然回复…※通常情况下，处于技能期间的单位皆持有阻回") or a full bar pause it. PRTS 异常效果 STUNNED "状态机尝试切换至STUN（无法攻击、释放技能、阻挡敌人类单位；敌人类单位无法移动）" and gamedata_const ba.stun "无法移动、阻挡、攻击及使用技能" name no SP effect; ba.frozen likewise. So a stun does not stop natural recovery. 洛洛 S2 自负此轭 (skill_table `skchr_rockr_2`; the chess status skillLevel 4 / elite 7): duration 40, SP cost 45 / 40, initial SP 15 / 20, ATK +40 % / +50 %, ASPD +45 / +60, funnel cap ×1.6 / ×1.7; ba.overdrive "技能进行到一半时触发额外效果"; "技能结束后晕眩过载持续时间的等长时间".
- **Duration — correct, unchanged**: the overload starts half-way (20 s) and the stun lasts as long as the overload ran (20 s after a full skill; an earlier end stuns for the part already overloaded — the triage measured 5.03 s for a stop 5 s past half-way, 0 before it); the data match skill_table at both levels.
- **Cause**: `SkillRuntime.tick` returned when the unit could not act (`!canAct`: stunned, frozen, levitated) before the natural recovery, so a stunned operator's time SP stood still — written as a rule in skills.js and docs/SIM.md ("no SP gain … while stunned").
- **Now**: the time SP recovers first (unless 阻回 `noSp` or a running timed skill, both unchanged), then a unit that cannot act skips its trigger checks and casts. A general rule — every stunned / frozen / levitated operator with time SP. 洛洛 after a full S2: +20 SP during the 20 s stun (was 0), recast 45 s after the skill end (elite 40) instead of 65 (60). Others that change: self-inflicted — 幽灵鲨 S2 肉斩骨断 (10 s stun after the skill: +10 SP; recast 45 / 43 s after the end instead of 55 / 53), 圣聆初雪's 圣山的祝福 (4 s self-freeze after the saved knock-out: +4 SP unless a skill runs — S2 holds 阻回 anyway); 雷蛇 S2 反击电弧 is hurt-SP (受击回复, which already counted hits while stunned) — no change. Enemy stuns / freezes on operators: 粉碎攻坚手 / 组长 and 码头水手 / 水手长 (every Nth attack), 流泪小子 (鸭爵, 3.5 s), “巨大的丑东西” (its knock-out blast), 杰斯顿·威廉姆斯, 重弩突袭者 (直击), 乌顶巨角卢鲁 (角力对决) and the leaders' stuns (胄's 剑 / 锤 blasts, tentacles 5 s), the 神经损伤 burst on operators (10 s), cold-on-cold freezes (3 s) and 浮空 — the stunned operator's SP keeps filling, its casts wait until it can act. Attack SP (no attacks while stunned) and hurt SP are unchanged; units that are not deployed recover nothing.
- [ASSUMED]: none (the rule is PRTS's; frozen / levitated follow the same SP-cooldown text).
- Docs: skills.js header, docs/SIM.md §7.1.
- Tests: `test/sim/feedback3-stunsp.test.js` (洛洛 S2's data against skill_table for both pieces; a full S2: a 20 s stun, +10 SP after 10 s of it, 20.5 after the stun, recast 45 s after the skill end; frozen: +1/s; 阻回: nothing; an operator that fills its bar while stunned casts only after the stun), `test/sim/combat.test.js` (the stunned operator neither attacks nor casts, SP +1.9 over 1.9 s — the old assertion was "no SP").

Refused: unknown code (`ROOM_NOT_FOUND`), a solo room or both seats taken (`ROOM_FULL`), a player of that room (`ALREADY` — nobody switches to spectating in place), a session whose own room is in a match (`ROOM_STARTED`, as `room.join`); repeated by the spectator it is idempotent, and from another lobby room it leaves that room first (as `room.join`). `room.state` carries `spectators: [{ playerId, name, connected }]` (always present, `[]` without). A spectator is **no player**: never in `seats`, never counted for the 1–4 players, the ready count or the `room.start` gate, never host (`migrateHost` reads `seats` only), and never keeps a room alive — when the last human leaves, the room closes and its spectators get `room.closed {empty}` (players who left themselves get nothing, as before).
