### 22.1 Back-facing operators play their fall (GitHub #25) — `render/units.js _wantsBack / _syncModel / die / revive / _acquireSpine / _dropActor / dieClipDur`, `render/spine.js SpineActor.die / dieClip / deployElapsed`

- **Seen** (public issue #25, "部分干员被击倒后在部署状态时仍有攻击动作"; the screenshot: 跃跃 seen from behind under its redeploy ring, still in its attack pose, while 拉普兰德 beside it lies down; the reporter also saw it on 拉普兰德, 异德 (缄默德克萨斯), 斯卡蒂 and 德克萨斯, "并非稳定出现" — all five have a Back skeleton without a Die clip). **Real** in 0.1.0 and 0.1.1, drawing only (the sim had the operator down; it never attacked): an operator deployed facing UP is drawn with its Back model (§9, research 07 §5.5), and 131 of the mode's 135 Back skeletons (data/assets.json) have no Die clip — only 薄绿, 玛恩纳, 松果 and 预备干员-重装 have one, while every operator's Front skeleton has one. Not every time: only operators facing UP.
- **Official**: PRTS 卫戍协议/帮助 says a knocked-out operator stays on its tile as a "倒地干员" (§21.24); which model the official client lies it down with is in no source. The project's asset plan already had the rule — research 07 §5.4 "`die`: `Die` (if the Back model lacks it, swap to the Front model for the death animation)", docs/ASSETS.md Roles `die: null` — but the renderer never implemented it. [ASSUMED] the official client does the same (a Back skeleton without a Die clip cannot show a fall), and the four Back skeletons with a Die clip fall with it.
- **Cause**: `UnitView._wantsBack()` ignored whether the unit stands; `SpineActor.die()` found no clip, returned 0 and changed nothing, so the attack / skill / idle loop playing at the knock-out went on under the ring for the whole respawn timer.
- **Now**:
  - `_wantsBack()`: facing UP with a Back model, while the unit stands; a dead or knocked-out one keeps the Back model only when that skeleton has a Die clip (`dieClipDur` of its manifest entry), so the other 131 fall and lie with the Front model (unmirrored, as facing UP).
  - `die()` and `revive()` mark the view, and its next `update()` — after every event and the snapshot of that frame (render/app.js) — runs `_syncModel()`: the model the state wants is loaded when it is neither shown nor loading, and swapped in place like a direction change (no avatar diamond in between). A death and a redeploy handed out in the same frame load nothing: a 突袭 jump (retreat + redeploy in one tick), a 不屈 revive (from the death hook), a backlog delivered after a hidden tab.
  - The Front model replays the state: the Die clip where it would be (`dieT`), a knocked-down one holding its end; `die(true)` and a view built for a unit already down start on the held end (the 联防 entry `FORCED_EXIT`, a field entered late, the re-entry from the field meta after an overflowed hidden-tab backlog). Its first frame gets the knocked-down grey (`_dropActor` drops the cached tint; a swapped-in model used to keep the previous model's tint state). A view that gets no `down` entry (summons and devices) fades like one facing RIGHT — a forced-out operator facing UP lies down like a knocked-out one (§22.15): the Front model's Die clip times the fade (`_fallDur`).
  - The redeploy (a `deploy` event: timed, 不屈, 阿戈尔 — every `Battle._deploy` — or a revive seen only in the snapshot) brings the Back model back; a deploy clip the Front model is playing goes on on the Back model where it is (`SpineActor.deployElapsed`).
  - Safety net: until the Front model is there, or when it fails to load, the Back model holds still — `SpineActor.die()` on a skeleton without a Die clip stops whatever looped and holds the first frame of its idle clip (frozen when it has none either), and nothing restarts an attack on a dead actor. The 20 summon / enemy skeletons without a Die clip (e.g. 浊心斯卡蒂's 海嗣) now fade holding still instead of looping [ASSUMED look].
  - Unchanged model choice: operators facing RIGHT / DOWN / LEFT, enemies and summons (no Back models in the manifest: `_wantsBack` is false for them; only the safety net above changes how their idle-only skeletons die), the redeploy ring, the sim. Nothing changes in render/interp.js or battle/runner.js: `die` / `deploy` are state events every client stage keeps (runner `keepsState`, game.js `keepEarly`, interp `isCosmeticEvent`), and b.snap `down` / `fieldMeta` rebuild the view of an operator already down; both now reach the model swap.
- [ASSUMED]: the Front model for the fall and the knocked-down pose (above); the Back skeleton's own Die clip when it has one; the held first idle frame of a skeleton without a Die clip.
- Tests: `test/render/issue25-downback.test.js` (fake PIXI with the real manifest and asset helpers: the manifest counts; the five operators of the report lie down with their Front model's Die; an UP knock-out mid-attack — the Back model holds still, then the Front model's Die is held under the ring, greyed, every acquire paired with a release; RIGHT unchanged; the redeploy back to the Back model with the deploy clip carried over; the snapshot's DIE anim and a snapshot revive; a death and a redeploy in one frame load nothing; a view built for an operator already down and the 联防 entry lie on the held end; a withdrawn one fades like a RIGHT one; 薄绿 keeps its Back Die clip; the SpineActor safety net; enemies and summons unchanged); `downring`, `feedback1f-down-tile`, `unitview`, `forms`, `test/ui/unitFacing.test.js` unchanged.

### 22.2 突袭's idle redeploy lands where it can attack (#51) — `content/bonds/addon/battle.js raidPoll / raidTile / raidReach / raidTargets`, research 02 §3.18

- **Seen** (GitHub issue #51, on 0.1.0): a 突袭 member idle for 10 s was redeployed even when no landing tile let it attack an enemy, so it hopped every 10 s between useless tiles; and the idle trigger always went for the most advanced enemy, even when another ground enemy could have been reached. **Real** (reproduced on 0.1.1): with one speed-0 enemy that no landing tile reaches (it stands on plain floor, and a RIGHT-facing melee member reaches it only from that tile or the floor tile behind it), the member jumped at 10 / 20 / 30 / 40 s between (10,9) and (10,8) beside it and never attacked; a second, reachable enemy was ignored. On the five real runs of the F5 test (`test/sim/feedback1f-downed-tile.test.js`) 26 of 60 jumps landed with no enemy in range — e.g. three members jumping to one enemy whose two reachable tiles the first two took.
- **Official**: the bond text (activity_table bondInfoDict / effectInfoDataDict; PRTS 盟约记录, no note under it) "【突袭】干员10秒内未进行攻击或技能就绪时，若范围内没有敌人，则保留技力立即再部署至一名地面敌人周围" names no tile rule; whether an official jump can land out of range is not known (the reporter: the text "按字面允许落在不能立即攻击的位置").
- **Cause**: the covers-the-target requirement of `raidPoll` bound only the 技能就绪 trigger (`if (!idleOk && !tile[2]) continue;`), and `raidTile` scored covering tiles first but still returned a non-covering one when none covered — so the idle trigger jumped to its first candidate whatever the tile.
- **Now** (the owner's decision — the reporter's proposal; stricter than the 「优先」 of our first reply on #51: a covering tile or no jump, never a fallback landing): both triggers jump only to a landing tile from which the member's attack range (its own grid + 攻击距离 rangeExtend, turned by its direction; a huge enemy counts on any tile it occupies) covers the chosen ground enemy. The poll walks the candidates in the existing order — its own field's ground enemies (another field's only while its own has none), the most advanced (least remaining path) first, at most the 8 most advanced — and takes the first one that has such a tile (the nearest within 2 tiles, the facing-RIGHT tie-break as before). When none has one the member stays where it is: its idle time keeps counting and the 0.25 s poll looks again, so it jumps as soon as an enemy can be reached. After a jump the idle time restarts from the landing (then from each attack). Unchanged: never onto a knocked-out operator's tile (§21.24), never into the 深水区 (§21.3, `grid.canStand`), SP kept, a real redeployment (部署时 effects), the ATK / HP bonus.
- **Cost**: a search that finds nothing now repeats every 0.25 s, so it is cheap: `raidTile` looks only at the tiles a range offset leads back to from the enemy's body (before: a 25-tile scan building a range per tile), and a player's members share one sorted candidate list per poll (rebuilt after a jump). On the heavy 2-player boss field of `test/sim/robustness.test.js` (≈ 130 enemies, 72 on the ground) with 18 members that never reach anything — every poll searches — the bond costs +5.8 µs per tick, as much as 0.1.1's +5.5 µs with its jumps every 10 s (the requirement alone on the old scan: +29.6 µs). Whenever a covering tile existed the landing tile is the old one (200 000 random cases: grids, 攻击距离, directions, huge bodies).
- **Players notice**: a member with nothing it can reach waits on its tile instead of hopping; it passes over an unreachable leading enemy for one it can hit; when several members go for one enemy and its reachable tiles are taken, the rest wait. The five F5 runs: 35 jumps instead of 60, none out of range.
- [ASSUMED]: "never a useless landing" (the text says only 再部署至一名地面敌人周围; issue #51); the cap of 8 candidates (a member waits when the 8 most advanced ground enemies are all out of reach — the 技能就绪 path's bound since 0.1.0, kept for the cost); the target order and the 2-tile search (research 02 §3.18, now written as above). Issue #49 (a passive skill never counts as 技能就绪) is unchanged and separate: it decides when a member may jump, this section where it lands.
- Tests: `test/content/bonds_addon.test.js` "突袭 #51" ×2 (no reachable landing: neither trigger jumps in 45 s, then a reachable enemy → both jump within one poll; an unreachable leading enemy is passed over for the second, which it attacks, and the next jump comes the idle time after its last attack); the other 突袭 tests (`bonds_addon`, `feedback1-water`, `feedback1f-downed-tile`, `facing-invariance`) unchanged.

### 22.3 阿戈尔's devour skips members it has already knocked out (#33 item 1) — `content/bonds/core.js devour`, `content/items/battle.js deploymentOf` (exported through `content/items.js`), `kits/tier5.js` (乌尔比安 S3, #33 item 2)

- **Seen** (GitHub #33 item 1, "阿戈尔阵营复活机制看起来不会触发"): the 5-tier revive works on its own (5 distinct 阿戈尔, one lethal hit each: the first 3 come back on their tile at full HP, normal and 联防 battles alike), but the usual food chain used it up at t = 0. One row, all facing right, 浊心斯卡蒂 → 乌尔比安 → 幽灵鲨 → 海霓 → 深巡 → 隐现: the battle-start devour knocked out 幽灵鲨, 海霓 and 深巡, the revive brought each straight back (all 3 charges), and 乌尔比安's marks still pending on them knocked them out a second time — down until their 70 / 80 s redeploy, no revive left for the fight (联防 battles alike).
- **Official** (PRTS 卫戍协议：盟约 下半/PRTS盟约记录, 阿戈尔 备注): "标记按付与顺序触发【吞噬】效果，目标首次被击倒后解除自身被付与但还未触发的【吞噬】效果"; the 5-tier "前3名【阿戈尔】干员首次被击倒时立刻复活" (bonds.json `max_free_respawn_cnt` 3), implemented as "受益者因移动之外的原因退场时下次部署的再部署时间和费用归零". So a devour knock-out is a first knock-out and spends a revive, and the revived member takes no further mark.
- **Cause**: `devour()` skipped a mark only while its target or marker was dead (`!t.alive || !m.alive`); a member revived in the same pass was alive again, so its pending marks fired. 埃芒加德's revives went the same way (it revives in place: the next pending mark knocked the revived unit out again and spent a second charge), and so did 不屈's 立刻重新部署 (the redeployed unit was knocked out again in the same instant, where 不屈 does not fire twice, and stayed down).
- **Now**: when the marks are placed, the pass records each target's deployment (`items/battle.js deploymentOf`, the deploy counter plus the in-place revives — exported for this). A mark is skipped once its target is off the field or in another deployment, i.e. was knocked out during the pass — whether the 5-tier revive or 不屈's 立刻重新部署 redeployed it or 埃芒加德 / M3茧甲 revived it in place. The marker side is unchanged since 0.1.0 (PRTS describes only the target): a marker off the field gives no further mark (withdrawn in 0.1.4: a marker knocked out during the pass still resolves its marks, §24.7), one knocked out and back in the same pass still gives its marks. The chain above: 浊心斯卡蒂's five marks resolve, the three members fall once each, every later mark on them is cancelled. (0.1.2 revived all three, spending the 3 revives at t = 0; since 0.1.3 they belong to the first 3 members by position, not to the first knocked out — here 幽灵鲨 comes back and 浊心斯卡蒂 / 乌尔比安 keep theirs for the fight, §23.20.) When nothing brings a devoured unit back during the pass (no 5-tier revive, 不屈 redeploy, 埃芒加德 or M3茧甲), the devour resolves exactly as before.
- **联防 (#33 item 3, not changed)**: the members knocked out twice at t = 0 were still down at the end of the 45–60 s own combat (70 s redeploy), so they entered every 联防 knocked out (forced out, §19.3) and the chain there formed without them. After this fix that is far rarer: with the 5-tier, the members the devour knocks out (up to 3) stand again and enter 联防 down only when the fight knocks them out once more (with 3–4 members no 阿戈尔 revive brings them back, as before). The order of the 联防 forced exit and the battle-start devour is forced exit first (§23.2).
- **乌尔比安 S3 (#33 item 2) — seen**: "乌尔比安3技能结束返回会清空阿戈尔阵营增益，转职突袭后疑似不会返回（未转职时三技能会在原地留下一个图标，转职后无图标）". **Official** (PRTS 乌尔比安 S3 备注; the chess skill is `skchr_ulpia_3`, skill range 6-1 = his own tile and the 6 ahead): the anchor's target is "自身所在地块（仅阻挡敌人时）>技能范围内距离自身最近的包含敌人的地块>技能范围内距离最远的地块"; the 【移动】 goes to the landing tile > the tile beyond it > his own tile, and "若目标地块不为当前地块，会在原地部署一个从不混淆的方向"; "【移动】后仅继承下列效果：技能进度、第二天赋叠加层数". **Cause**: the anchor search started one tile ahead, so while he blocked — typical right after a 突袭 landing next to the enemies — he threw ahead, walked off the enemy he blocked (the blast could miss it — with an enemy one tile ahead its 1.8 radius still reaches the one he blocks) and left the marker. **Now**: while he blocks an enemy the anchor lands on his own tile: the blast (radius 1.8) hits what he blocks, he stays put, no marker, nothing to return from. His own tile is a candidate only while he blocks ("仅阻挡敌人时"): without a blocked enemy the search starts one tile ahead, exactly as in 0.1.1, so a flyer over his tile does not keep him there. Considered and not taken: reading the own tile of the 6-1 range as distance 0 for the second rule. Measured with the triage's batch (8 real stages × 5 round templates × 2 seeds, 突袭 through 变形同构体 + 突袭手雷), before the 突袭 landing change (#51, §22.2) is merged: with 突袭, 56 of 84 casts used to move him off his landing tile, now none does — 82 of the 84 came while he blocked, the other 2 (act2 m03, tile (10,8)) have no standable tile ahead (pre-existing); without 突袭, 64 of 80 casts still move him, as before.
- **乌尔比安 S3 — not reproduced** (the triage, real data): no 阿戈尔 gain is cleared on the return. The devour's base ATK / block (`bond:egir:devour`, e.g. +399 ATK, +1 block), the 阿戈尔 HP and the 血脉的哺养 stacks are there before, during and after the S3 (`Battle.relocate` keeps every buff); what ends is the S3's own +40–60 % HP / +200–230 % ATK. With 突袭 he does return and the marker is drawn, both on the landing tile next to the enemies, not on his board tile (8 stages × 5 round templates × 2 seeds); with this fix a cast while he blocks there leaves no marker at all, as officially. **Deviation, kept**: officially the 【移动】 keeps only the skill progress and the second talent's stacks, so the official game drops the devour gains on the move; the remake keeps them through the 【移动】 and the return.
- [ASSUMED]: (1) a marker revived during the pass keeps the base ATK / block it gained at marking (a persistent buff, as through every knock-out since 0.1.0). (2) With the anchor on his own tile, 乌尔比安's 【移动】 does not try "the tile one beyond the landing" (he would otherwise step one tile forward).
- Tests: `test/content/bonds_core.test.js` (the chain in a normal and a 联防 battle: one knock-out and one revive each, all five standing, the next knock-out final; a marker revived by the 5-tier revive still gives its marks; a target 埃芒加德 revived in place takes none — one revive spent, not two; a target 不屈 redeployed takes none and stays standing), `test/content/kits_t5.test.js` (乌尔比安 S3 while he blocks: the anchor on his own tile, no move, no marker; a flyer over his tile, not blocked: the anchor ahead and the move, as in 0.1.1).

### 22.4 活性源石 stays on an enemy after it leaves the tile (#33 item 6) — `server/sim/content/devices.js` (`touchInfection`, `enterTerrain`, `terrainFor`, `tickMire`, `deepWaterDamage`), `content/enemies.js` (`rebirthCleanse`, `kitDeepsea`), `tools/build-data.mjs` (`special.mire`)

- **Seen** (public issue #33, other part 3: 「红地板图敌方单位经过后，红地板附带的效果会消失」): on 战场#04 an enemy lost the red floor's effect the moment it walked off it. Measured on v0.1.1 (the real stage): an enemy on (10,6) from 15.67 s to 19.67 s lost 4 × 70 HP; 12 s later it still had 99720 / 100000 HP and no ATK / ASPD bonus.
- **Official**: the red floor is 活性源石 `tile_infection`, the only one in the season's data — 战场#04 (`act1autochess_m04`) at (10,6), (11,6), (10,14), (11,14) and on the boss rows at (3,6), (4,6), (3,14), (4,14); level blackboard `damage` 70, `atk` 0.2, `attack_speed` 20, `duration` 300. PRTS 特殊地形 tile template (`特殊地形/trapper/tilesformat.json`): "部署于其上的我军和经过的敌军在{duration}s内每秒受到{damage}真实伤害，攻击力提升{atk×100}%，攻击速度增加{attack_speed}" (main-story stage pages: "在五分钟内"). The crisis-contract tag `global_tile_infection_1` 「目标：可控感染」 (PRTS 切尔诺伯格 6区废墟/历史合约): "踏过活性源石地块的敌人不再持续损失生命值" — without it an enemy that has walked over the tile goes on losing HP. Same-name effects do not stack (PRTS 作战机制: "同名buff的默认叠加策略buff只能表现出一个"). Its damage is 环境伤害 (PRTS 自然环境 lists 活性源石). A 重生 clears every buff but a whitelist (PRTS 特殊机制 §重生 "清空自身身上除白名单外所有Buff"; its 非首次标记 row: "如无特殊说明，也默认同常规Buff一样可被重生清除").
- **Cause**: `devices.js` read `duration` as a lifetime of the tiles (`terrainFor` switched them off after 300 s of battle time) and tied the effect to standing on the tile (`enterTerrain` removed it as soon as the unit's tile changed).
- **Now**: every tick a unit is on the tile (allies by their tile, ground enemies by their rounded position; flyers, hovering / levitated and hidden units never touch it, as before), `touchInfection` gives it the timed effect `terrain:infection` for `duration` s — 70 true damage per second, ATK +20 %, ASPD +20 — or, when it already carries it, gives it back its full time. Leaving the tile no longer removes it: an enemy that crossed it drains 70/s with ATK +20 % / ASPD +20 for 300 s from its last contact — longer than any own-field or 联防 battle lasts (≤ 230 s of battle time). All 12 ground legs of 战场#04 (own field, 联防 field, boss field) cross one of these tiles, so every ground enemy there carries it once past the tile. One effect per unit: a second pass or the next tile of a pair restarts its time and never adds a second effect (ATK +20 % once, 70/s, no extra tick). The tiles never switch off — the old lifetime only ever ran out on the boss / hidden fields (no time limit), at 300 s. An operator deployed on it is always in contact, so it drains until it is knocked out or withdrawn, past 300 s too (this follows from the [ASSUMED] refresh rule; v0.1.1 drained it the same way within the first 300 s). An operator moved off the tile (`Battle.relocate`: 乌尔比安's S3 move and return, 夕's 小自在) keeps the effect for its time — 乌尔比安 landing on (10,6) carries it home for 300 s (measured by the review); leaving the field (knock-out, withdrawal, 突袭's jump) drops it with every other buff. A 重生 (`rebirthCleanse`: 逐火 embers, 假想敌：再生's 傀儡, 锏, 守墓石像 …) clears it like any buff, and contact gives it again while the enemy stands on the tile. Kept through the 重生 (the first version of this fix), its 1 s ticks — each a hit of a husk's 特殊生命值 — killed a 深池逐火战士 ember that had crossed (11,6) at ~6 s instead of letting it stand up at 1 + 10 s (护卫's 火灰 at ~11 s, 傀儡 at ~16 s; found by the review). The damage itself is unchanged: true damage no unit deals (无来源), tagged `terrain` (环境伤害), a damage instance, not a 流失.
- [ASSUMED]: the time counts from the last contact (it is refreshed every tick on the tile) and the per-second rhythm runs on through a refresh — hence an operator on the tile drains past 300 s; contact gives the effect again to an enemy reborn on the tile; not 持续伤害 (no `dot` tag: PRTS 伤害分类's list of BUFF damage names 深水区, 源石污染区 and 活性源石风暴 but not 活性源石, so 锡人's 凋敝魂灵 does not raise it).
- **Checked**: no other terrain reads a `duration` — 沼泽 (layers, cleared on leaving), 深水区 (`sea_drown[enemy]` has none), 烟雾 and the blowers; 沼泽's interval and 深水区's damage kind were wrong, though (below).
